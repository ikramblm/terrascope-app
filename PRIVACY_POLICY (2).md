# TerraScope Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 26, 2026_

TerraScope ("the app") is a geography quiz game developed for Android and other
platforms. This policy explains what data the app handles.

## Summary

TerraScope does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app
works entirely offline.

## What TerraScope stores

The following information is saved **only on your own device**, using
Android's standard local app storage. It is never sent to us, to any server,
or to any third party:

- Your chosen display name (defaults to "Guest Explorer" until you change it)
- Game progress: scores, streaks, XP, unlocked titles, and settings
- Local notification scheduling state, used only to remind you about an
  active streak

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes this data, since nothing is stored
anywhere else.

## Permissions

TerraScope requests one runtime permission:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`)** — used only to show a local
  reminder when you have an active streak that's about to expire. You can
  decline this permission; the app works normally without it, you simply
  won't get streak reminders. No notification content is ever generated
  from or sent to a server.

## Sharing a Duel challenge

TerraScope lets you challenge a friend by sharing a short text code. That
code is generated and decoded entirely on your device (it contains only a
random seed, question count, your score, and your display name) and is
shared only through the share method you choose (e.g. your phone's own
share sheet). We do not see or store this code.

## Third parties

TerraScope does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting
SDKs, and makes no network requests. No data is shared with any third party
because no data leaves your device.

## Children's privacy

TerraScope does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

## Changes to this policy

If TerraScope's data practices ever change (for example, if a future update
adds online features), this policy will be updated and the in-app version
will be updated to match before that update is published.
