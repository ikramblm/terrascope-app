TerraScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

TerraScope ("the app") is a geography quiz game for Android. This policy explains what information the app handles, including what the ads shown in the app collect.

Summary: TerraScope has no accounts and no servers of its own, and it never sends your game progress anywhere. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and AdMob collects limited device information to serve and measure those ads (details below).

Information stored on your device only

The following is saved only on your own device, using Android's standard local app storage. We never receive it and it is never sent to any server:

  • Your chosen display name (defaults to "Guest Explorer" until you change it)
  • Game progress: scores, streaks, XP, unlocked titles, and settings
  • Local notification scheduling state, used only to remind you about an active streak

Uninstalling the app deletes this data, since it is stored nowhere else.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

TerraScope shows banner ads at the top of its main screens (never during a game) using the Google Mobile Ads SDK from Google AdMob. To serve, measure and protect against fraud in those ads, the SDK automatically collects and shares with Google:

  • Your device's IP address, which can be used to estimate your general location
  • Device identifiers, including the Android advertising ID and an app-set ID
  • Interactions with the app and ads, such as app launches and taps
  • Diagnostic information, such as app launch time, hang rate, and energy usage

This data is encrypted in transit. We do not receive this information, and we do not combine it with your game progress. How Google uses it is described in Google's advertising privacy page and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the app shows Google's consent form the first time it runs, and ads load only after you have made your choice. You can change your choice at any time from the same form when it is offered.
  • Advertising ID: on your device, go to Settings → Google → Ads to reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization. If you delete your advertising ID, ads can still appear but are not personalized to you.

Permissions

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — used only to show a local reminder when you have an active streak that's about to expire. You can decline it; the app works normally, you simply won't get streak reminders.
  • Internet and network state — used only by the ads SDK to load ads. The game itself plays fully offline.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android ad services — let the ads SDK read your advertising ID, described above, and use Android's built-in ad services.
  • Vibrate, wake lock, foreground service — standard permissions added automatically by the notification and ads libraries (for notification vibration and short background work while an ad loads). The app does not run any ongoing background service of its own.

Sharing a Duel challenge

TerraScope lets you challenge a friend by sharing a short text code. That code is generated and decoded entirely on your device (it contains only a random seed, question count, your score, and your display name) and is shared only through the share method you choose, such as your phone's own share sheet. We do not see or store this code.

Children's privacy

TerraScope is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us (below) and we will help. Ads shown in the app are restricted to general-audience content.

Changes to this policy

If TerraScope's data practices change, this policy will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will change before the new version of the app is published.
